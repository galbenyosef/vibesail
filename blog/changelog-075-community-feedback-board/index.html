Changelog 075: Community Feedback Board

Share ideas, report problems, and vote on what sailors want next — plus a clearer place to get in touch.

VibeSail now has a public Feedback board where sailors can share ideas, report problems, and help decide what matters most.

  • Browse the community board at vibesail.com/feedback.
  • Vote once per account on any open request — click again to clear your vote.
  • Sort by Top to see what sailors want most, or New for the latest posts.
  • Sign in or create an account when you are ready to add your own feedback.
  • Requests we have already shipped stay on the board with a Done badge, so progress is easy to spot.

Need something else? The Contact page at vibesail.com/contact gathers our community links and email in one place. Use Feedback for game ideas and bugs, and Contact when you want to reach us directly.
