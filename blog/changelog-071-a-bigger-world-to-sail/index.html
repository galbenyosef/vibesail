Changelog 071: A Bigger World to Sail

The sea has a fresh layout with more island variety, clearer routes, and more places to discover.

The VibeSail world now gives you more space to sail, explore, and choose your own route between islands.

  • Islands are spread across a broader sea, so journeys have more open water and better moments of discovery.
  • New island types give you more distinct places to discover, recognize, and navigate around while sailing.
  • Cargo ships now sail clearer lanes through the world, making the sea feel more alive without crowding the islands.
